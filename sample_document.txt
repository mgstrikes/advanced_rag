
Our company provides health insurance to full-time employees.
Employees can add eligible dependents to their health insurance plan.
The company covers a portion of the monthly insurance premium.

Employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave each year.
Leave requests should normally be submitted at least five working days in advance.
Managers approve leave requests based on team availability.

Employees can claim approved business travel expenses.
Travel expenses must be submitted within 30 days of completing the trip.
International travel requires approval from the regional manager before booking.
